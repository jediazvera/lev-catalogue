# Towards a Catalogue of Texts for the LEV Corpus of Judeo-Germanic and Judeo-Romance (1400–1800)

**Authors:** Javier E. Díaz-Vera and Teodoro Manrique-Antón  
**Affiliation (both authors):** Universidad de Castilla-La Mancha (UCLM), Spain (ROR: https://ror.org/05r78ng12)  
**DOI supplied by the authors:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24074204  
**Dataset status:** Research review draft. Confirm the DOI status against Zenodo's record before publication.

## What this deposit contains

- `LEV_Catalogue_Full_142_Records.csv` — all 142 English-language catalogue records, including context and research holds.
- `LEV_Catalogue_Core_132_Candidates.csv` — 132 provisional target-period candidates (not an independently verified final corpus).
- `LEV_Catalogue_Browse.html` — a user-friendly searchable catalogue; this is a downloadable HTML file, not a hosted website.
- `LEV_Catalogue_Annotated.xlsx` — curated working workbook with research review decisions.
- `LEV_Methods_Metadata_and_Quality.zip` — complete structured evidence package: data dictionary, bibliography, controlled vocabulary, JSON-LD and Data Package metadata, scope exclusions, validation flags, evidence sources, editorial policies and research-review report.

The collection is **an inventory of textual candidates, not 142 full-text transcriptions**. It includes Yiddish (50 records), Judeo-Italian (40), and Judeo-Spanish (52). Original language names and citations are retained for provenance while descriptive fields are in English. Historical identification and dates remain provisional where flagged.
